# RALLY — Brand System

RALLY is where **football culture meets nightlife and fashion**. It helps people find where others are watching the match and show up together. The brand is bold, social, unisex, cultural, confident — never corporate, never a stats app.

**Tagline:** *Find your game. Find your people.*
**Manifesto:** *We don't just watch the game. We rally for it.*
**Vibe words:** stylish · social · football · unisex · confident · cultural · bold

## Colour palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Lime (primary) | `#8ACE00` | Primary CTAs, highlights, the brand mark. Pair text in **ink**, not white. |
| Ink | `#0F0F0F` | Text, inverted blocks, the "night" side of the brand. |
| Paper | `#F3F0E8` | Default background (warm off-white, NOT pure white). |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` | Cards on paper. |
| Line | `#E3DDD0` | Hairline borders on paper. |
| Pink | `#FF3E9A` | Accent — "big one", share, energy. |
| Blue | `#2A5BFF` | Accent — student / info. |
| Purple | `#7B3FF2` | Accent — expat / culture. |
| Flame | `#FF5A1F` | Accent — hardcore / heat. |

Rules: light **paper** base by default; use solid **ink** blocks for drama and contrast (featured match, the "lowdown", splash). One accent per surface — don't rainbow. Lime is the hero; the others are spice.

## Typography

Three roles. The board's licensed fonts are listed first; the **free substitutes** are what the prototype ships with (swap to licensed for production).

1. **Headlines / wordmark** — *New Order Bold* → free sub **Archivo Black**. Heavy grotesque, UPPERCASE, tight tracking, set big and loud. This carries the brand.
2. **Editorial accent** — *PP Editorial New Italic* → free sub **Instrument Serif (italic)**. The "chic" voice: taglines, pull-quotes, "did you know", flourishes. Lowercase, italic. Use sparingly for contrast against the heavy grotesque.
3. **UI / body** — *Suisse Int'l* → free sub **Inter**. Everything functional. Labels are small, **bold, UPPERCASE, letter-spaced** (`tracking ~0.18em`).

Signature move: a heavy grotesque headline with one word dropped into the italic serif (e.g. **WHO'S WATCHING** *tonight?*).

## Logo & icon

- **Wordmark:** `RALLY` in Archivo Black, uppercase, tight tracking.
- **App icon:** lime gradient square (`#A6E61A → #6BAA00`), near-black **R**, a small **pink** accent dot top-right, faint white "energy arc" lines bottom. Full-bleed (stores apply the rounded mask).
- **Full/marketing logo** (optional): location-pin + crowd + football mark over the wordmark.
- Lockup: wordmark above the italic-serif tagline *find your game. find your people.*

## Layout & motion principles

- Editorial, generous whitespace, strong left alignment, big type. Magazine, not dashboard.
- Rounded-2xl/3xl cards and **full-round pill buttons**.
- Jersey/scoreboard motif: set kickoff times and counts in the heavy grotesque, like shirt numbers.
- Tags = solid colour pills, uppercase, tiny.
- Subtle paper grain; keep motion quick and physical (`active:scale` taps, small pop/rise).
- Imagery (when real photos exist): raw, candid, analogue/film-grain, real fans, nightlife — confident and human.

---

## Voice

Confident, warm, a bit cheeky, football-literate. Short. Banter over corporate. "Find your people", "the lowdown", "be the first", "Skål". Never hype-deck buzzwords.

## Don'ts
- No pure-white clinical SaaS look; no single-accent dark-mode "AI default".
- Don't position as dating, social network, or stats app.
- Don't put "World Cup"/"FIFA" in the legal name or use FIFA logos (trademark).
- Don't overuse the serif — it's seasoning, not the dish.
